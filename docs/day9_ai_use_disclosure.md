## Limitations of AI-generated code and documentation

- AI can produce plausible but incorrect code, tests, citations, mappings and
  security claims.
- Tests suggested by AI may encode the implementation rather than the intended
  requirement and may omit adversarial, integration or production conditions.
- Small synthetic datasets and generated models can give misleading performance
  impressions and do not establish generalisation, fairness or safety.
- AI cannot convert the user-space kernel simulation into real kernel-level
  protection by description, nor convert the local hash-linked ledger into a
  deployed blockchain.
- AI review of repository artifacts cannot authenticate screenshots, external
  CI runs, cloud infrastructure or human authorship without independent records.
- Dependency and vulnerability results become stale and must be rerun.
- Generated prose may overstate completion unless scope and limitations are
  manually checked against the official brief.
